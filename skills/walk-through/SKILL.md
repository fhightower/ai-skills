name: walk-through
description: Use when the user wants to go through a list one item at a time — "/walk-through", "walk me through these", "let's go through them one by one", "take these one at a time" — either a list they point to (an earlier message, a file, a pasted list, an issue) or, by default, the most recent list you posted. Presents one item per turn and stops for questions or actions before moving on.
---

This skill walks through a list of items (either a list referenced by the human or the most recent list posted by the agent) one item at a time.

To get started, state the number of items in the list you are planning to walk through and then present the first item.

After presenting an item, pause for the human to respond. Handle responses as appropriate. If the response is "Next", show the next item in the list.
